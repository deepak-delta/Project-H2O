1 · Welcome

Water Delivery is a digital notebook and ledger for water-can businesses. It records every delivery, every empty can that comes back, every rupee given on credit and every payment, so you always know who owes you money and who has your cans.

Who uses the app

Owner

Runs the business. Sees everything: all clients, money, stock, staff and reports. Adds clients, staff and products.

Delivery staff

Records deliveries, payments, empty cans and expenses. Sees only their own work for today and the orders given to them.

Client

A shop, home or office you supply. Optional: can order water, see their balance, cans and payments in their own app.

Three ideas to know

BalanceWhat a client owes you. Shown in red as Owes ₹…. If they paid in advance it shows in green as Advance ₹…. Every sale adds to it; every payment reduces it.
Cans with a clientYour reusable cans sitting at the client.Cans with client = cans they already had + full cans delivered − empty cans collected − damaged cans taken back
Nothing is erasedEvery entry is kept with who made it and when. A mistake is fixed with a reversal or a correction that shows in the history, so the books always add up.

The app works like a normal phone app once installed, works with weak or no internet (see section 14), and keeps your data safe on the server with a backup every night.

2 · Getting started

Install the app on your phone

Open the app's link in your phone's browser. The app offers to install itself the first time.

iPhone (Safari):

  1. Tap the Share button in Safari's toolbar.
  2. Scroll down and choose Add to Home Screen.
  3. Open Water from your Home Screen.

Android (Chrome): tap Install when the app asks, or open Chrome's menu ⋮ and choose Install app / Add to Home screen.

If you opened the link inside WhatsApp, the app asks you to open it in Safari or Chrome first. Installing works only from the real browser.

Create your business (owner)

  1. On the login screen tap Create an account.
  2. Enter your business name, your name and phone number, then tap Continue.
  3. Choose a 6-digit PIN. You will log in with your phone number and this PIN.

Staff and clients do not sign up. You create their logins for them (sections 10 and 6).

Log in

Enter your phone number and 6-digit PIN, then tap Log in.

After your first login the app offers Face ID / fingerprint. Turn it on and next time you unlock with your face or finger instead of typing the PIN. You can turn it on later in More → Login & security.

After 5 wrong PINs the account locks for 15 minutes (longer if it keeps happening), so nobody can guess a PIN. The owner can set a new PIN for staff and clients.

First-day checklist

  1. Products: add what you sell, e.g. 20L can, bottle crate (section 11).
  2. Stock: enter how many filled and empty cans you have (section 7, Add stock).
  3. Clients: add each client with their price, and the cans and money they already have from your old notebook (section 6).
  4. Staff: add your delivery people (section 10).

3 · Home screen (owner)

Your business today, at a glance. It updates by itself within seconds when staff record something, so you don't need to refresh.

TodayOrders, orders still pending, today's sales, and cans delivered (with empties collected).
MoneyCash/UPI collected today, credit given today, expenses today, net cash today, and the total outstanding with all clients.
CansFilled and empty cans in your stock, and cans out with clients.

Tap a number to open the matching list. A yellow warning appears if deliveries took your filled stock below zero: count your cans and correct the stock (section 7).

Bottom bar: Home, Orders, the big + for a new delivery, Clients, and More (stock, staff, reports, payments, cash flow, products, settings).

4 · Recording a delivery

The most common job: cans go to a client, empties come back, money is paid now or later. One screen, a few taps.

  1. Tap the big + in the bottom bar.
  2. Choose the client. Clients you served recently are at the top. Search by name, owner, phone number or type. Results update with every letter.
  3. Cans delivered: use − and + or type the number for each product. The price shown is this client's price. If a client has no price yet for a product, the app asks for it once and remembers it.
  4. Empty cans collected: how many empties came back. Same as delivered fills it in one tap. The app shows how many cans the client has, and warns if you enter more than that.
  5. Damaged cans (only when it happens): tap Some cans came back damaged, enter how many and the charge per damaged can. The client pays this; the cans leave their count and go to your Damaged stock.
  6. Payment: Paid full, Part paid (type the amount) or Credit, and how they paid: Cash, UPI, Bank or Other.
  7. Check the summary and tap Confirm delivery.

Payment and credit

The summary at the bottom shows the total, what was received now, what is added to credit, and the client's balance after this delivery.

  • Paying more than the bill clears older dues automatically.
  • If this delivery takes the client over their credit limit, you see a warning (you can still save it).
  • Damaged-can charges appear as "Includes 2 damaged cans" in the total.

After saving

You see the order number, totals and the client's new balance. Tap Send receipt on WhatsApp to send the client a ready-made message with what was delivered, paid and still due. New order starts the next delivery.

No internet? Confirm anyway. The delivery is saved on the phone and sent when the connection returns (section 14).

5 · Orders

Orders for later, and giving them to staff

When a client asks for water to be delivered later (or you want a staff member to do it):

  1. Tap + and choose the client.
  2. Switch from Deliver now to For later.
  3. Enter how many, choose Today or Tomorrow, and under Who delivers? pick yourself or a member of staff.
  4. Add a note if needed (e.g. "back gate before 10am") and tap Create order.

The staff member sees the order on their Today screen and gets a notification if they turned notifications on. The bill is made only when the order is delivered, for what was actually delivered.

The Orders screen

Tabs: Today, Pending (not delivered yet) and All. Search by client name or order number. Each order shows the amount and its status:

PendingPlaced by a client in their app; waiting for you.
ConfirmedAccepted, to be delivered.
Out for deliveryOn the way.
Paid / Part paid / On creditDelivered, and how much of it has been paid.
CancelledNot delivered, or a delivery that was undone.

An order's page

  • Delivered by: change which staff member delivers it.
  • Out for delivery marks it as on the way; Deliver opens the delivery screen with the ordered quantities filled in. You can deliver part of an order; the rest stays open.
  • Payments taken for the order are listed; a payment entered by mistake can be Reversed here.
  • Cancel order for an order not yet delivered. For a delivered order the button is Cancel delivery: the sale is taken off the client's balance and the cans go back to stock. Money that was really received stays on the client's account and counts against their dues.
Cancelling asks for confirmation and a reason, and is kept in the history.

6 · Clients

Every shop, hotel, home, office or person you deliver to.

The list shows each client's type, phone, cans with them, and their balance (Owes or Advance).

  • Search by client name, owner name, phone number or type. Results update as you type each letter.
  • Filters: All, Owes money, Has cans, Inactive.
  • Tap a client to open their page.

Adding a client

Tap + Add on the Clients screen.

  1. Client name and type: Store, Home, Individual, or Other (type your own word, e.g. Hospital, Apartment).
  2. Price for this client: what they pay for each product. Every client can have their own price; the last client's prices are filled in to save typing.
  3. Optional: owner name, phone (for calls and WhatsApp), address, credit limit (you're warned when a delivery takes them over it), notes.
  4. Cans already at this client: cans they have from before you started using the app. Leave 0 for a new client.
  5. Already supplying this client? Add the amount they already owe from your old notebook.
  6. Tap Add client.

Only the owner can add or edit clients. To change a client's details or prices, open the client and tap the pencil ✎. New prices apply to future orders; past orders keep the price they were made at.

The client page: their ledger

At the top: Balance, Cans with client and the last delivery date. Below that:

OrderStart a delivery for this client.
PaymentRecord money received against their balance.
EmptiesRecord empty cans collected without a delivery.
CallPhone the client.
Send payment reminderSends a reminder to the client's app (only if they have app access and owe money).

Money history lists every sale, payment and discount with the running balance after each one, like a notebook page. Cans history lists every delivery, empty return, damaged can and correction. Tap a line to open its order.

Payments, empty cans and can corrections

  • Record payment: type the amount (or tap Full balance), choose Cash, UPI, Bank or Other, and Save payment.
  • Collect empty cans: for each product, how many empties you picked up, then Save.
  • Correct (next to Cans with client, owner only): if you count the cans at the client and the real number is different (for example cans they had before you started), enter the real count, Review change, and confirm. The change is recorded as a correction with the old and new numbers.

Client app access

A client can have their own login to order water and see their balance, cans and payments (section 13). At the bottom of the client's page, under Client app:

  • Turn it on with the client's phone number and a 6-digit PIN, and share the PIN with them.
  • Reset PIN if they forget it; Turn off to remove their access.

Deactivate client hides a client you no longer supply. Their history is kept, and you can reactivate them from the Inactive filter.

7 · Stock

Open More → Inventory. For each product you see where every can is.

FilledFull cans ready to deliver.
EmptyEmpty cans in your store, waiting to be refilled.
With clientsYour cans at clients' places. Tap to see who has them.
DamagedBroken cans kept aside.

Deliveries and returns update stock automatically. Use the buttons only for things that happen in your store:

  • Add stock: new cans bought or received (filled and/or empty).
  • Refill: empty cans filled at the plant (empty → filled).
  • Damaged / lost: cans broken or missing, taken from your empty or filled cans.
  • Correct count: after a physical count, enter the real numbers. The difference is recorded as an adjustment.

Cans with clients lists every client holding your cans, most first, with their phone and last delivery date. Use it to plan collections.

8 · Payments, cash flow and expenses

Payments

More → Payments lists every payment received, newest first: client, method, order and time. Tap a payment to see it. If it was entered by mistake, tap Reverse payment: an opposite entry is added and the amount goes back onto the client's balance. The original stays in the history, marked reversed.

Cash flow and expenses

More → Cash flow & expenses shows money in and out for Today, Yesterday, the last 7 days, this month or last month:

  • Money in (water sales, old dues collected, deposits, other income), money out (expenses), net, and cash in hand.
  • Totals by category and every entry.

Payments from clients appear here automatically. To add an expense or other income, tap + Add, choose Expense or Other income, pick a category (Fuel, Water purchase, Vehicle, Salary, Maintenance, Electricity, Other expense), enter the amount and method, and choose Today or Yesterday. An entry made by mistake can be opened and reversed with Reverse entry.

Open More → Reports. Every report can be downloaded as a CSV file for Excel or Google Sheets.

Business summaryEverything in one report for 7 days, 1 month, 3 months or 1 year. Downloadable as PDF and CSV.
Daily salesSales, money collected and cans for each day.
Monthly salesThe last 12 months, month by month.
Who owes moneyEvery client with dues, biggest first, with when they last paid and a WhatsApp button.
Cans with clientsWho has your cans.
Stock movementOpening stock, every kind of movement, and closing stock, for each product.
Payments receivedEvery payment in a period, for your records.
Cash flow & expensesMoney in and out by category (section 8).

Business summary

Choose 7 days, 1 month, 3 months or 1 year at the top. The report shows:

  • This period: sales, collected, cans delivered and deliveries, and change in dues (sales not yet paid).
  • Money received by Cash, UPI, Bank and Other; cash flow and expenses by category.
  • Cans: delivered, empties collected, damaged cans taken back, damaged or lost in stock, refilled.
  • Right now: total owed to you and cans with clients today, and stock now.
  • Owner and staff: each person's deliveries, cans and money collected.
  • Top clients by sales, and who owes you the most.

Download PDF makes an A4 document to keep, print or send on WhatsApp. Download CSV gives the same figures as a spreadsheet.

Sales are counted when cans are delivered, not when an order is placed. Change in dues = sales − money collected: positive means clients now owe you more than at the start of the period.

10 · Delivery staff

Each delivery person gets their own login, so you know who delivered what and who collected which money.

Add a member of staff

  1. Open More → Delivery staff and tap + Add.
  2. Enter their name and phone number. A 6-digit PIN is suggested (tap New for another).
  3. Tap Add staff and give them the phone number and PIN to log in.

See each person's day

The staff list shows each person's deliveries and cans today and their staff expense. Tap a person to see:

  • Cash to hand over: cash they took minus cash they spent. This is what they should give you at the end of the day.
  • Deliveries, cans delivered and empties back, money collected (with UPI shown separately), expenses.
  • Orders given to them and what they did today, line by line.

What staff can and can't do

  • Can: record deliveries, collect payments, collect empties, add expenses, deliver orders you give them, see clients' balances and cans.
  • Can't: add or edit clients, see reports, stock, other staff or past days. Their screen starts fresh every morning.

11 · Products and settings

Products

More → Products. Add each thing you sell: name, size in litres, whether the container is returnable (20L cans are; bottle crates usually aren't), and an optional deposit amount. Returnable products are tracked as cans with clients; non-returnable ones are not. Prices are not set here: each client has their own price (section 6).

More menu

Your business details, and links to Inventory, Delivery staff, Reports, Payments, Cash flow & expenses, Products, Notifications and Login & security, plus Log out.

Login & security

  • Face ID / fingerprint: the phones that can unlock with your face or finger. Remove a phone you no longer use.
  • Change PIN: enter your current PIN and a new one.

Notifications

Open More → Notifications and turn them on for this phone. The owner is told about new orders from clients, orders a client cancels, and when filled cans run low. Staff are told when an order is given to them. Clients are told when their order is accepted and on the way, when it is delivered (with their new balance), when a payment is recorded, and about payment reminders. Notifications are set per phone. On iPhone they work only after the app is added to the Home Screen.

12 · Guide for delivery staff

Log in with the phone number and PIN your owner gave you. Your screen shows only today. It starts fresh every morning.

Today

  • Cash to hand over: cash you collected minus cash you spent. Give this to the owner at the end of the day.
  • Your deliveries, cans delivered, empties back, money collected and expenses today.
  • To deliver: orders the owner gave you. Tap Deliver, check the cans, empties and payment, and confirm.
  • What you did today: every delivery, payment, empty collection and expense you recorded.

Bottom bar: Today, Clients, + for a new delivery, Expenses, More.

  • New delivery: tap +, choose the client and record it exactly as in section 4.
  • Clients: search and open a client to see what they owe and how many cans they have, then Order, take a Payment, collect Empties, or Call them.
  • Expenses: fuel or anything you paid during the day: + Add, choose the category, amount and how you paid.
If a client is missing, ask the owner. Only the owner adds clients.

13 · Guide for clients

Your water supplier gives you a login (phone number and PIN). Open the app link, install it (section 2), and log in.

Home

  • The big Order water button.
  • Balance: what you owe your supplier (or your advance), and Statement for the full history.
  • Cans with you and your last delivery.
  • Your recent orders and payments.

Order water

  1. Tap Order water.
  2. Choose how many of each product, Today or Tomorrow, and add a note if needed.
  3. Tap Send order. You pay for what is actually delivered.

You can cancel an order until your supplier accepts it.

  • Orders: all your orders, or only those not delivered yet, with how much of each is paid.
  • Payments: every payment your supplier recorded.
  • Statement: every delivery and payment with your running balance, and every can delivered and returned.
  • Account: notifications (order on the way, delivered, payment received, reminders), Login & security, Log out.

14 · Working without internet

Deliveries happen where the signal is weak. The app keeps working.

  • Clients, prices, balances and recent orders are kept on the phone, so you can open them without internet.
  • Deliveries, payments, empty collections and expenses recorded offline are saved on the phone and sent automatically when the connection returns.
  • A banner says "You're offline. Showing saved information." and a bar shows how many entries are saved on this phone, waiting to sync. Tap it to see them; when everything is sent it says Everything is synced.
  • Each entry is sent exactly once, even if the phone retries several times, so nothing is billed twice and nothing is lost.
An entry saved offline gets its order number after it syncs. Logging out keeps waiting entries; they are sent when the same person logs in again. Don't clear the browser's data while entries are waiting.

15 · Questions and answers

I entered a wrong delivery.Open the order and tap Cancel delivery, then record it again correctly. The cancelled one stays in the history, marked cancelled.
I entered a wrong payment.More → Payments, open the payment, Reverse payment.
A wrong expense.Cash flow, open the entry, Reverse entry.
The cans at a client don't match.Count them, then on the client's page tap Correct and enter the real number.
My stock doesn't match.Count your filled and empty cans, then Inventory → Correct count.
A client paid in advance.Record the payment as usual. Their balance shows as Advance and the next deliveries use it up.
A client broke a can.When delivering, tap Some cans came back damaged, enter how many and the charge per can. The client is billed and the can goes to Damaged stock.
Can I delete something?No. Money and can records are never erased. Every change is a new entry, so the history always explains the balance.
A staff member forgot their PIN.Ask the owner to set a new PIN for them.
A client forgot their PIN.The owner opens the client and taps Reset PIN under Client app.
A price changed.Edit the client (pencil ✎) and change their price. Future orders use the new price; past orders keep theirs.
Is my data safe?Logins use a PIN or Face ID / fingerprint, each person sees only what they need, and the database is backed up every night.
